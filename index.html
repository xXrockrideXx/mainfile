Search syntax
  • HELIX or *SLANT* matches anywhere in the record
  • 201?? or 203* matches machine numbers
  • 203*;315*;50101 this or that (plain number = exact)
  • 2* & helix+ & 5c this and that, as many & as you like (spaces ignored)
  • 2* & helix & !slant ! means not
  • 2* & helix+ ; 93* mix them: ; splits the groups
  • /regex regular expression on the whole record
  • Saved: tap to filter by your saved searches (Denoms, Areas, your own groups). Same group = or, different groups = and. Edit to add your own.
  • Machines shows only machine numbers. Banks shows only banks (10101, 10103 → bank 101). Both on: machines indented under their bank. Tap any number for its details.
  • denom:1c exactly 1c · multi:denom · multi:game · flag:ebs EBS (not PPS) · flag:pps EBS PPS
  • bank:830-899 banks in a range, bank:510 one bank, bank:511+ from 511 up. Mix with the rest: bank:150-199 & 1c
  • |201*;305?? or table: 201* side-by-side compare
  • /extract:^(\d{3})01$=>$1 pulls parts out of machine numbers
